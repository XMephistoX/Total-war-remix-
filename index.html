270 BC · Rome, Carthage and the kingdoms of the Mediterranean

How to play

Mouse: left click selects, drag to box select, shift adds. Right click ground to move, right click an enemy to attack. Double right click to run. Right drag to draw a formation line (sets width and facing); white markers show exactly where each soldier will stand. Double click selects all units of a type. Ctrl+1-9 saves a group, 1-9 recalls it. WASD pans, Q/E rotates, wheel zooms, middle drag orbits.

Tactics: hit units in the flank or rear, hold high ground, keep your general close, don't tire your men by running everywhere, and use spears against cavalry. Watch the morale list on a unit to see why it is wavering. A flag blinking white means that unit is close to breaking.

Controls

Mouse: left click selects, drag to box select, shift adds. Right click ground to move, right click an enemy to attack. Double right click to run. Right drag draws a formation line. WASD pans, Q/E rotates, T/B or PageUp/PageDown tilt, wheel zooms, middle drag orbits. Space pauses, R toggles run, H halts, G guard mode, Esc or left click on empty ground deselects, L line mode, C centers, Ctrl+A selects all, +/- speed. Double click a unit to select every unit of that type. Ctrl+1-9 saves a group, 1-9 selects it, press twice to jump the camera there.
