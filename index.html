Battle test · 270 BC

How to play

Mouse: left click selects, drag to box select, shift adds. Right click ground to move, right click an enemy to attack. Double right click to run. Right drag to draw a formation line (sets width and facing). WASD pans, Q/E rotates, wheel zooms, middle drag orbits.

Tactics: hit units in the flank or rear, hold high ground, keep your general close, don't tire your men by running everywhere, and use spears against cavalry. Watch the morale list on a unit to see why it is wavering.

Controls

Mouse: left click selects, drag to box select, shift adds. Right click ground to move, right click an enemy to attack. Double right click to run. Right drag draws a formation line. WASD pans, Q/E rotates, T/G tilts, wheel zooms, middle drag orbits. Space pauses, R toggles run, H halts, L line mode, C centers, Ctrl+A selects all, +/- speed.
